# Pollee Inc — User Manual

**Real Democracy, one transaction at a time.**

Pollee is a real-time democratic participation platform. Members vote on polls, debate issues in discussion boards, delegate their vote to trusted peers, and earn reputation for constructive participation. A layered hierarchy of moderators — Infomarians, Constituency Managers, and Master Franchisers — keeps the conversation civil and the process trustworthy.

This manual is organised by the platform's role hierarchy. Each section explains what that role can see and do. Most users only need to read the section for their own role, plus the shared "Everyone" section at the top.

## 1. Everyone — Shared Features

These features are available to every signed-in user, regardless of role.

### Navigation

The top navigation bar adapts to your role. Items you cannot access are hidden. A theme and language toggle (light/dark, multiple languages) sits at the right of the page.

### Incident Reporting

Any user can file an incident report from the **Report** item in the navigation bar.

- Choose the level to forward the report to: **Board**, **Infomarian**, or a **specific user**.
- Set a priority (low, medium, high, urgent).
- Add a description of the incident.
- Reports are tracked through statuses: open → in progress → resolved → closed.
- Moderators can add resolution notes when closing a report.

### Direct Messages

The **Messages** item opens your private conversations.

- **Starting a conversation:** Moderators (Infomarians and above) can start new conversations with any member. Members reply to conversations initiated with them.
- **Thread view:** Select a conversation from the left-hand list to view the full message thread. Newest messages appear first.
- **Attachments:** You can attach `.mp3` and `.wav` audio files, plus images and documents, to any message. Attachments are stored privately — no public URL, so access follows your app's permissions.
- **Comment share consent requests:** When another member asks to share one of your discussion comments, a consent request appears at the top of the Messages page. You can **Accept**, **Reject**, or **Reply** (which opens the conversation with the requester). An email is also sent to you with a link back to Messages.

### Profile

Every user has a **Profile** page showing:

- Display name, avatar, and bio.
- Reputation score and badge (see [Reputation System](#10-reputation-system)).
- Voter ID and account verification status.
- Vote history (polls you have voted on).
- Delegations you hold (votes others have delegated to you) and delegations you have made.
- Conduct strikes, if any.
- **Declaration of Interest** (Infomarians only) — any financial, professional, personal, or political interests that could create a conflict when moderating a poll. Edits are saved with explicit confirmation; if a save fails, a red error message appears under the text box.
- **Australian Bank Details** — greyed out and non-editable with a "Transaction Layer Suspended" notice.

### Important Documents

The Home page and the Vote page link to three governing documents hosted on pollee.net:

- **Code of Conduct** — community behaviour rules.
- **Model Rules** — the constitutional framework.
- **Code of Practice** — voting and moderation practice.

## 2. Members & Voters

Members are verified, registered users aged 18 or over. This is the default role after registration is complete.

### Voting on Polls

1. Open the **Polls** page (Home) to see all active, approved polls.
2. Click a poll card to open the **Vote** page.
3. The Vote page shows the poll question, description, the discussion board, the budget summary, and the voting form.
4. Select an option (Yes, No, or RTS — Reconsider The Submission) and submit your vote.
5. A **$0.55 AUD** transaction binds your vote to a constituency account. The payment breaks down as:
   - $0.30 to the Infomarian
   - $0.10 to Pollee Inc
   - $0.10 to the local constituency
   - $0.05 GST
   - Any amount above $0.55 is a tip to the Infomarian.
6. You can **change your vote** at any time before the poll closes. The system records the history but counts only your latest vote.
7. Polls automatically close **30 days (720 hours)** after creation.
8. Each verified vote earns you **+5 reputation**.

### Discussion Boards

Every poll has a discussion board (visible on the Vote page, below the poll header).

- **Post a comment:** Type your comment and accept the responsibility agreement before posting. Comments are published immediately for members.
- **Reply:** Use the reply button on any comment to start or join a threaded discussion.
- **Claps:** You can applaud (clap) a comment to show appreciation. Clapping awards a reputation point to the comment author.
- **Share to Member:** You can forward a comment to another member privately. When the share feature is reinstated, the originating commenter must consent before the comment is delivered (see [Direct Messages](#1-everyone--shared-features)).
- **Forward content to Infomarian:** Members can forward relevant content (URLs, files, text) to the Infomarian for the poll's discussion. Forwarded items enter the Infomarian's content library for review.

### Delegations

You can delegate your vote to a trusted peer.

- **Open delegation:** Your delegate carries your vote on every poll.
- **Specific-poll delegation:** Your delegate carries your vote on one poll only.
- Delegations pass through a verification step before they take effect.
- You can revoke a delegation at any time.
- Your delegate's votes count your delegated weight (shown as "delegated votes" in results).

### Results

The **Results** page lists every poll with a running tally. Click a poll to expand it and see:

- Total votes (including delegated weight).
- Verified votes.
- Pending votes (awaiting verification).
- Transaction count.
- A results chart breaking down votes by option.

Live results also appear on the Vote page while a poll is open.

## 3. Public Participants

Users without a validated account can still participate in discussions as **general public** participants.

- You must provide a display name when commenting.
- Your comments are marked with a "public" author type.
- You **cannot vote** — voting requires a validated member account aged 18+.
- You **cannot share comments** to other members — sharing requires a registered account.
- Comments from public participants are subject to the same moderation as member comments.

To become a full member, validate your account on your Profile page and complete registration.

## 4. Junior Members (12–17)

Members aged 12 to 17 have limited participation:

- **Can** read and post in discussion boards (with a parent/guardian note acknowledging participation).
- **Can** view live and final poll results.
- **Cannot vote** — voting requires age 18 or over.
- The Vote page shows a notice explaining that junior members can discuss but not vote.

Members under 12 cannot participate in discussions or voting.

## 5. Infomarians

Infomarians are the platform's front-line moderators. They are assigned to one or more constituencies and hold a moderation level (local, state, or federal). The **Infomarian Dashboard** is their home base.

### Dashboard Overview

The dashboard shows four summary cards:

- **Total Earnings** — accumulated earnings from vote transactions ($0.30 per verified vote).
- **Pending Comments** — comments awaiting moderation or flagged.
- **Pending Media** — comments with media attachments awaiting review.
- **Moderation Level** — your authority scope (local, state, federal).

### Moderation Queue

The **Queue** tab is the primary moderation workspace. It lists all comments and media items awaiting review. For each item you can:

- **Approve** the comment.
- **Reject** the comment (with a reason).
- **Flag** the comment for further review.

A red badge on the navigation bar and the Queue tab shows the count of pending items.

### Poll Review

The **Poll Review** tab lists newly created polls awaiting moderation approval.

- **Approve** a poll to publish it to all members. Approving a poll awards **+10 reputation** to the poll's creator.
- **Reject** a poll with a reason.

### Assigned Polls

The **My Polls** tab shows every poll you are assigned to moderate. From here you can open the poll's discussion, view its budget, and monitor live results.

### Budget Management

The **Budgets** tab lets you enter and lock cost figures for budget lines on proposals.

- Enter a cost figure and confidence interval (±%) for each budget line.
- **Lock** a figure to mark it as official — locked lines cannot be edited until unlocked.
- Set the **budget envelope** (the audited legislative limit) or skip it to derive the envelope from the sum of line totals.
- **Finalise** the budget once every line has a figure. Editing any line reverts the budget to draft.
- Nominated **budget editors** (members) can co-edit figures, but locking, the envelope, and finalising stay restricted to Infomarians and above.

### Comments & Media Moderation

- **Comments tab:** Review and moderate all discussion comments across your assigned polls.
- **Media tab:** Review comments that include media attachments (images, audio, video). Approve or reject the media.

### User Management

The **Users** tab lists members in your constituency. You can:

- View member profiles and reputation.
- **Issue a strike** to a member for conduct violations. Each strike is incident-specific.
- **Revoke a strike** (incident by incident) when a strike is lifted. Revoking one strike does not reset the member's overall reputation or other strikes.
- View a member's vote history and delegation activity.

### User Support

The **Support** tab is a helpdesk for member queries. Members can raise support requests, and you respond directly through the conversation thread.

### Task Assignment

The **Tasks** tab lets you assign tasks to other Infomarians or to yourself.

- Task types: moderation, user support, content creation, poll management.
- Set priority (low, medium, high, urgent) and a due date.
- Track task status: pending → in progress → completed / cancelled.

### Content Library

The **Library** tab is your personal evidence and content store.

- Add content items (URLs, uploaded files, text, images, audio, PDFs).
- Tag items for searchability.
- Attach library items as **evidence** to poll discussions.
- Review content **forwarded by members** from poll discussions — forwarded items enter as "pending" and you approve or reject them into your library.

### Earnings Tracker

The **Earnings** tab shows your earnings from vote transactions over time.

### Register New Users

Infomarians can register new users from the dashboard or the Admin panel. The registration flow sends a confirmation code to the new user's email, which they must paste back to confirm their account (see [Account & Registration](#9-account--registration)).

### Declaration of Interest

On your Profile page, maintain a **Declaration of Interest** — any financial, professional, personal, or political interests that could create a conflict when moderating a poll. When you are assigned to a poll, an AI-powered conflict-of-interest scan compares your declaration against the poll content and flags any potential conflicts before the assignment is confirmed.

### Help

The **Help** tab opens the Infomarian help guide with detailed instructions for every moderation task.

## 6. Constituency Managers (Franchise Managers)

A Constituency Manager (Franchise Manager) owns a single constituency (franchise) and manages its Infomarians and polls. The **Constituency Manager Dashboard** is their home base.

### Dashboard Overview

Four summary cards show:

- **My Infomarians** — count of Infomarians in your constituency.
- **Active Polls** — polls in your constituency currently open.
- **Total Polls** — all polls ever created in your constituency.
- **Constituency Status** — active, inactive, or suspended.

### Tabs

- **Overview:** Quick actions (manage Infomarians, create a poll, view results) and constituency information (name, primary postcode, state, contact phone).
- **Infomarians:** List of Infomarians in your constituency with their status. Add new Infomarians via the Constituency Admin page.
- **Polls:** All polls in your constituency with vote counts. Create new polls via the Admin page.
- **User Registration:** Information about the self-registration process. New users register themselves and are confirmed by email.
- **Help:** The Constituency Help guide.

### Managing Infomarians

From the **Constituency Admin** page (linked from the dashboard and the Admin panel), you can:

- Create and edit Infomarian profiles (name, expertise areas, assigned postcodes, moderation level, bio, declaration of interest).
- Suspend or reinstate Infomarians.
- Assign Infomarians to specific polls (with the AI conflict-of-interest scan running before assignment is confirmed).

### Creating & Managing Polls

Constituency Managers have full access to the **Admin** panel (see [Super Admin — Admin Panel](#8-super-admin)) for creating polls, managing them, verifying votes, and banking — scoped to their own constituency.

## 7. Master Franchiser

The Master Franchiser oversees all constituencies, Infomarians, and polls across the platform. The **Master Franchiser Dashboard** is their home base.

### Dashboard Overview

Four summary cards show platform-wide totals:

- **Total Constituencies**
- **Total Infomarians**
- **Active Polls**
- **Total Votes** (verified)

### Tabs

- **Overview:** Quick actions (manage constituencies, manage polls, view all results) and system status (active constituencies, pending moderation count).
- **Constituencies:** Every constituency with its postcode, state, and status. Manage constituencies via the Constituency Admin page.
- **Infomarians:** Every Infomarian across all constituencies with their ID and status. Edit any Infomarian's profile (expertise, postcodes, moderation level, bio, declaration of interest) directly from this list.
- **Polls:** Every poll across all constituencies with vote counts. Create polls via the Admin panel.
- **Moderation:** All pending and flagged comments across the platform, with author and status.
- **Users:** The full user directory (see [User Directory](#8-super-admin)).
- **Help:** The Constituency Help guide.

### Managing Constituencies

From the **Constituency Admin** page, the Master Franchiser can:

- Create new constituencies (franchise name, primary postcode, postcodes served, state, owner email, contact phone).
- Edit constituency details and bank account information (Yes, No, RTS destination accounts).
- Suspend or reinstate constituencies.

## 8. Super Admin

The Super Admin (`ac@acproductiondesign.com`) has unrestricted access to every feature in the platform, including every dashboard and the full Admin panel.

### Admin Panel

The **Admin** panel is the operational hub for poll and vote management. It has five tabs:

#### Create Poll

- Enter the poll question (title) and description.
- Select the constituency (franchise) that owns the poll.
- Choose the poll level: **Local** (postcode-restricted), **State** (state-restricted), or **Federal**.
- For local polls, enter the eligible postcodes (comma-separated).
- Enter up to **two** voting options. A compulsory third option **"RTS"** (Reconsider The Submission) is added automatically.
- Add **budget lines** — labelled cost items that the Infomarian will later price.
- Polls automatically close after **30 days (720 hours)**.
- New polls are created with `moderation_status: pending` and must be approved by an Infomarian before they appear to members.

#### Manage Polls

- See every poll with its status (active/closed), moderation status (pending/approved/rejected), vote counts, and assigned Infomarians.
- **Approve** or **Reject** pending polls. Approving awards +10 reputation to the creator.
- **Assign** Infomarians to a poll (opens the assignment dialog with the AI conflict-of-interest scan).
- **Open the Budget** panel to enter and lock budget figures.
- **Close** or **Reopen** a poll.
- **Delete** a poll (with confirmation).

#### Verify Votes

- Lists every vote with `status: pending`.
- For each vote, review the transaction reference, amount, bank, date, voter, Infomarian ID, delegation status, delegated vote count, transaction description, and the $0.55 payment breakdown.
- **Verify** a vote to mark it as counted. The tip amount (anything above $0.55) is calculated and stored.
- **Reject** a vote with a reason.
- A red badge on the tab shows the count of pending votes.

#### Banking

- Configure the **Pollee Inc** bank account (BSB, account name) used for 55c registration validation deposits.
- Configure the **vote destination accounts** — separate account numbers for Yes, No, and RTS votes. Vote transactions are directed to these accounts based on the vote option.

#### User Directory

- Search and browse all registered users.
- View each user's role, reputation, verification status, and activity.
- Edit user records (role, profile details).
- Issue and revoke strikes (incident-specific).

### Register New User

From the Admin panel or any dashboard, the Super Admin can register a new user. The flow sends a confirmation code to the new user's email (see [Account & Registration](#9-account--registration)).

### Moderation Alerts

The **Moderation Alerts** page (visible to Infomarians and above) consolidates all pending moderation items — comments, media, and polls — in one queue with quick actions.

## 9. Account & Registration

### How New Users Join

1. An Admin or Infomarian starts the registration from a dashboard or the Admin panel.
2. The new user's email is entered and a **random confirmation code** is emailed to them.
3. The new user pastes the code back into the registration form to confirm their account.
4. The new user completes their profile: full name, date of birth (DD/MM/YYYY), constituency, and accepts the terms.
5. Registration is complete once the profile is saved and the email is confirmed.

New users cannot register themselves from a standalone sign-up button — registration is always initiated by an Admin or Infomarian.

### Login

- Sign in with email and password, or with Google.
- Forgot password and reset password flows are available from the login page.
- If your profile is incomplete (missing date of birth or last name), you are redirected to the New User Registration page to finish setup.

### Language Preference

Your chosen interface language is captured during registration and login and saved to your profile. The app supports multiple languages including English, French, German, Spanish, and Dutch.

## 10. Reputation System

Reputation reflects your contribution to the community. It influences your privileges and standing.

### Earning Reputation

| Action | Reputation |
|---|---|
| Verified vote | +5 |
| Approved poll (as creator) | +10 |
| Receiving a clap on your comment | +1 |
| Constructive comments and delegations | Tracked |

### Losing Reputation

- **Strikes** for conduct violations reduce your reputation.
- Each strike is incident-specific and can be revoked individually by an Infomarian or above.
- Revoking a strike removes only that strike's effect — it does not reset your overall reputation or other strikes.

### Reputation Levels

Your reputation score maps to a badge shown on your profile and next to your comments. Each reputation level displays its own icon. Higher reputation signals a trusted, active member of the community.

### Suspended Users

If you receive strikes that suspend your account, a red notice appears on discussion boards explaining the suspension and showing the most recent strike reason. Suspended users cannot post comments or vote until the suspension is lifted.

## 11. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Poll** | A question put to members for a vote. |
| **RTS** | Reconsider The Submission — the compulsory third option on every poll. |
| **Constituency (Franchise)** | A geographic unit (by postcode) that owns polls and has its own Infomarians and bank accounts. |
| **Infomarian** | A moderator assigned to one or more constituencies with a moderation level (local, state, federal). |
| **Master Franchiser** | The overseer of all constituencies and Infomarians. |
| **Constituency Manager** | The owner of a single constituency. |
| **Delegation** | Entrusting your vote to another member, either for all polls or one specific poll. |
| **Clap** | A public show of appreciation for a comment; awards reputation to the author. |
| **Budget Envelope** | The audited legislative limit on a proposal's spending, set by an Infomarian or above. |
| **Budget Line** | A labelled cost item within a proposal's budget, priced by an Infomarian or nominated editor. |
| **Declaration of Interest** | A disclosure of potential conflicts, required of Infomarians and scanned before poll assignment. |
| **Strike** | A conduct violation recorded against a member, incident-specific and individually revocable. |
| **Voter ID** | A unique identifier assigned to each verified member for vote binding. |
| **Transaction Layer** | The bank-transaction binding that ties a vote to a constituency account. Currently suspended. |
